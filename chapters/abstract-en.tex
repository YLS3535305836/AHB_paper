With the rapid development of integrated circuit technology worldwide, the intelligence level of portable electronic devices continues to rise, leading to increasing demands for fast charging technology in terms of charging power and speed.
The flyback AC/DC switch-mode power supply, known for its excellent isolation, simple design, and low cost, is widely used in the medium and low-power fields. However, flyback converters have certain limitations in light-load efficiency and switching frequency, which fail to meet the growing market demand.

This paper addresses the issues of low efficiency at light loads and limited switching frequency in traditional flyback converters. To explore further possibilities for flyback converters, a non-symmetric half-bridge flyback converter chip based on a secondary-side feedback structure is designed for medium-power fast charging applications.
In order to fully utilize the advantages of the non-symmetric half-bridge flyback converter, the system studies the topology and proposes a series of control strategies and key circuit modules aimed at achieving high-frequency, high-efficiency performance across a wide output voltage range and full load.

Under extremely light load and no-load conditions, unnecessary modules are turned off in burst mode to reduce standby power consumption. The switching frequency is reduced, and at light load, a zero-voltage resonant valley switching (ZV-RVS) mode is employed, introducing reverse magnetizing current to achieve soft switching of the power transistor.
In heavy-load conditions, critical conduction mode is used to control the asymmetric complementary conduction of the power transistors to meet high output power demands. Additionally, a valley-locking module is designed to eliminate the frequency-jumping issue within the power overlap range, and a wide output voltage compensation structure is incorporated to ensure correspondence between secondary-side feedback voltage and load current for different rated output voltages, satisfying multi-mode control requirements.
To further exploit the potential of the non-symmetric half-bridge flyback converter for high power density, high conversion efficiency, and high reliability, this paper presents the following innovations:

1.Precise valley conduction technology for ZV-RVS mode: To further improve the system efficiency in ZV-RVS mode, a precise valley conduction technique is proposed to reduce switching losses and current spikes influenced by the resonant voltage at the half-bridge node. This technology improves the valley detection scheme in quasi-resonant technology and eliminates delay errors in the driving module through advanced conduction and approximation strategies, reducing the maximum error to ±15ns, ensuring precise conduction at the lowest point of the resonant waveform.

2.Dynamically calibrated demagnetization time control technology: To optimize high-efficiency control strategies for the non-symmetric half-bridge flyback converter, a dynamic compensation circuit for the low-side power transistor's conduction time is introduced. By adjusting the slope of the ramp voltage, the demagnetization time of the inductive load is gradually overlapped with the conduction time, avoiding conduction losses and reliability issues caused by excessively long or short conduction times, ensuring high efficiency under multiple control modes.

The designed converter chip is based on SMIC's 0.18μm BCD process, with an input voltage range of 90V to 265V and a rated output range of 5V to 20V with a load current range of 0A to 5A. Simulation tests show that, under the multi-mode control strategy and the application of key technologies, the circuit significantly improves system conversion efficiency at a maximum output power of 100W. The peak conversion efficiency reaches 91\%, with a load regulation of 0.8\%, and a line regulation of 0.62\%, meeting the required performance specifications.
